# Interview Story

I built a telecom data engineering platform to practice the full lifecycle of operational data. I generated reproducible source datasets, added a quality gate before transformation, built a Customer 360 gold layer, and loaded the results into PostgreSQL. For real-time processing, I added Kafka producers and consumers and a PySpark Structured Streaming job using event-time windows. Airflow handles orchestration, dbt models the warehouse mart, and Prometheus/Grafana provide monitoring configuration.
